The Hidden Gem

A walking tour through the quieter, more beautiful, and aesthetic corners of the Acropolis

About this tour

Hi my name is Alex, and this is a carefully chosen walking route that avoids the biggest crowds and the noise of the city. It focuses on atmosphere, aesthetics, history, philosophy, and small discoveries.

Perfect for people who already know the main sights and are looking for something more personal, authentic, and special.

Precious tips for your next days in Athens will also be given in written form.

Itinerary: A mystical and aesthetic walk around the Acropolis.
